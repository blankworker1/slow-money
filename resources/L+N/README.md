# L+N: Letters and Numbers 

A series on records, names and numbers: how people came to be counted, and how they might live as people who are known instead.

1. [The Ledger Has Always Been the Frontline](01-the-ledger-has-always-been-the-frontline.md) — barter, legibility, and the record as the real story of money.
2. [Letters and Numbers](02-letters-and-numbers.md) — the map: what a ledger needs, and what a life does not.
3. [Unenumerability: Becoming Economically Inactive](03-unenumerability-becoming-economically-inactive.md) — the personal strategy.
4. [The Integer Is Not a Number](04-the-integer-is-not-a-number.md) — from oral law to the alphanumeric cage, ending in polite refusal.
5. [No Names, No Numbers](05-no-names-no-numbers-1.md) — fiction: secret cells in a world of total surveillance.
6. [No Names, No Numbers #2: The Joker's Tale](06-no-names-no-numbers-2.md) — fiction: the game the cells play.

The game itself, with printable prompt cards and a die net, is in [game/JT](../../game/JT/README.md).
