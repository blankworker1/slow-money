# Slow Money Roadmap

Work still to do on the Slow Money series. Completed items stay listed, ticked, as a record.

## Done

- [x] Manifesto, FAQ, Handbook, Glossary and Roots
- [x] Four age tracks and five practices
- [x] Licence, logo and homepage
- [x] The Test, with its third module, the Family Ledger

## Next

- [ ] **Printables.** A set of printable PDFs so the practices can happen on paper, at the table. See the list below.
- [ ] **Pilot.** Three or four families try the Kitchen Table and Money Memories before the series is promoted. Their feedback shapes the next revision.
- [ ] **Consistency pass.** Check every link, make sure the ground rules read identically wherever they appear, and align terms with the Glossary.
- [ ] **Italian translation.** Start with the Glossary to settle key terms (for example, the chronicle as *libro di famiglia*, the slate as *lavagna*), then the rest of the series.

## Later

- [ ] **Getting help page.** Where to turn when a money conversation needs outside support. It should point first to Slow Money mediation (how this works is still to be decided), then to trusted local individuals, not general national organisations.
- [ ] **Group and school session plans.** Ready-to-use sessions for teachers and community groups: one per age track, plus a mixed-age session built around Money Memories.
- [ ] **CONTRIBUTING.md.** How to fork the series for a local group, how to contribute translations, and, once it exists, how to take part in a GitHub Discussions area.
- [ ] **Fine-tune The Test.** Review the questions after the pilot, and write local versions of the law-specific items for countries other than Italy.

## Printables

Each printable is a single page (A4 or A5) that works in black and white, so it can be printed at home or in a library.

### Core
- Ground rules card
- One-page introduction to Slow Money, to hand to family members

### The Kitchen Table
- Host sheet for running a first conversation
- Question cards for the three rounds (memories, habits, hopes), including the children's questions

### Money Memories
- Interview sheet with space for notes and a consent box
- Then-and-now worksheet (prices, wages and hours of work)

### The Combined Wage
- Income sources worksheet, including the hidden wage
- Ten-token game board for children
- Weekly shop and weekly wage worksheet

### The Family Ledger
- Sorting card: the table, the slate, the chronicle and release
- Slate template
- Chronicle pages, with a title page, designed to be bound

### The Heirloom
- Heirloom worksheet: what we received, what we keep, what ends with us
- Heirloom Letter paper and envelope label

### Ages
- Children's activity sheet: saving jar label and needs-and-wants sort

### The Test
- Paper version with a scoring sheet, for anyone who would rather not take it online
