Slow Money

Intergenerational financial literacy, starting at the kitchen table.

Before anyone opens a bank account, they already know what money means. They learned it at home, by watching the people who fed them.

Slow Money treats every family as an economy, and financial understanding as something handed between generations. It offers ideas, practices and a short history for families to understand money together, and to choose on purpose what they pass on.
